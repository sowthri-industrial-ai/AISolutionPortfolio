Refinery Digital Twin

Azure-native digital twin for petroleum distillation, grounded in DWSIM steady-state simulation.

Status

Phase 1 foundation complete. Stages 2-6 and Features 1, 2, 3, 5 in progress.

Overview

A locked DWSIM Petroleum Distillation flowsheet (12-stage column plus thermal oil heating loop) is solved every 30 seconds. Snapshots flow into Azure Data Explorer, an ISA-95 twin ontology indexes them, Foundry agents reason with MCP-served tools, and an ADX dashboard plus Power BI plus Omniverse 3D render the operator experience.
